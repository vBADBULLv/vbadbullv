Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. Who is responsible

Controller for this website and the vBADBULLv app is the operator of the vBADBULLv channels (“vBADBULLv”, “I”, “me”). Contact: vbadbullv@gmail.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers (a) this website and (b) the vBADBULLv app – a personal desktop tool that uploads my own gameplay clips to my own accounts on YouTube and, once connected, TikTok, Instagram and Twitch. The app is used only by me; it has no public user accounts. Anyone who signs in to it (for example during a platform's app review) is covered by this policy in the same way.

3. This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you open a page, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and time of access in server logs to deliver the site and keep it secure – see the GitHub Privacy Statement. This website itself sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking and loads no external fonts, scripts or embeds. Legal basis: legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. Data the app accesses

  • Access and refresh tokens that a platform issues after you sign in on its official OAuth page.
  • Basic account information (channel or account ID and name), used only to confirm that the correct account is signed in.
  • Video files and metadata (title, description, tags, visibility) that I choose for an upload.

The app does not access passwords, contacts, messages, comments, followers or subscribers, analytics, payment data or any data of other users.

5. How the data is used and shared

The data is used for one purpose only: uploading the selected video to the selected account and confirming the right account is used. Videos and metadata are sent directly from my computer to the respective platform (Google/YouTube, TikTok, Meta/Instagram or Twitch). No data is sold, rented, shared with other third parties, used for advertising or used to train AI models. Legal basis: your consent given on the platform's sign-in screen (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.

6. Storage and retention

Tokens are stored only locally on my own computer (for example in a file named token.json). They are never uploaded to this website, a code repository or any server. Account information and API responses are not stored beyond the running upload. Tokens are kept until access is revoked or deletion is requested; they are then deleted without delay – at the latest within 7 days of a deletion request and within 30 days after access has been revoked.

7. YouTube API Services

The vBADBULLv app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app's access to your Google/YouTube data at any time via the Google security settings page.

vBADBULLv's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. TikTok, Instagram/Meta and Twitch

The same rules apply to these platforms: only tokens are stored locally, uploads go directly to the platform, no data is shared. Their own policies apply to data they process: TikTok Privacy Policy, Meta Privacy Policy (also for Instagram), Twitch Privacy Notice. The app follows the Meta Platform Terms and the platforms' developer terms.

9. Deleting your data

Step-by-step instructions for revoking access and requesting deletion are on the Data Deletion page. You can also simply e-mail vbadbullv@gmail.com.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

11. Children

The app and this website are not directed at children under 16.

12. Changes

If the app or the law changes, this policy will be updated; the date at the top shows the current version.

13. Contact

vbadbullv@gmail.com – see also the Contact page.
